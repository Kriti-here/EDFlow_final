## 2. Statutory Analysis (Regulations 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 & 18)

### Regulation 2: Definitions & Scope
* **Regulation 2(1)(a) Ã¢â‚¬â€ "Act":** Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (42 of 1999).
* **Regulation 2(1)(b) Ã¢â‚¬â€ "Authorised Dealer" (AD):** A person authorised as an authorised dealer under Section 10(1) of the Act.
* **Regulation 2(1)(c) Ã¢â‚¬â€ "Export Declaration Form" (EDF):** The form given at the **Annex** to these regulations.
* **Regulation 2(1)(da) Ã¢â‚¬â€ "resident individual":** A person resident in India who is a natural person (inserted via Second Amendment, Nov 2, 2026).
* **Regulation 2 Ã¢â‚¬â€ "Services Include Software":** In the regulatory architecture of FEMA 23(R)/2026-RB, "services" expressly include software exports.
* **Regulation 2(1)(f) Ã¢â‚¬â€ "Specified Authority":**
  * *(i) In respect of goods:* Commissioner of Customs in the Domestic Tariff Area (DTA); Development Commissioner in a Special Economic Zone (SEZ).
  * *(ii) In respect of services:* An **Authorised Dealer (AD Bank)** in DTA; Development Commissioner in an SEZ.
  * *(iii) In respect of software:* An **Authorised Dealer (AD Bank) OR Software Technology Parks of India (STPI)** in DTA; Development Commissioner in an SEZ.
  *(Crucial statutory fact: STPI is explicitly retained under Regulation 2(1)(f)(iii) as a Specified Authority for software in DTA alongside AD banks, giving software exporters the option to approach either authority. This is mirrored in Section 5 of the official EDF Annex itself).*

---

### Regulation 3: Declaration of Exports & Deemed Submission
* **Regulation 3(1) [Goods]:** Exporters of goods must submit an EDF specifying the full export value. For EDI ports, filing of the shipping bill constitutes deemed EDF submission.
* **Regulation 3(2) [Services & Software]:** Exporters of services must submit an EDF specifying the full export value to the Specified Authority within **30 days from the end of the calendar month** in which the invoice is raised. Consolidated monthly filings covering all invoices raised during that month are expressly permitted.
* **Regulation 3(2) Provisos (Deemed-Submission Framework):**
  * **First Proviso (a) Ã¢â‚¬â€ Resident Individuals:** A service exporter who is a **resident individual** (natural person) is **deemed to have submitted the EDF** upon providing required payment-receipt information to their Authorised Dealer for crediting their account. **No monetary ceiling applies to resident individuals.**
  * **Second Proviso (b) Ã¢â‚¬â€ Non-Individuals (Ã¢â€šÂ¹10 Lakh Limit):** A service exporter other than a resident individual (Pvt Ltd, LLP, Partnership firm) is **deemed to have submitted the EDF** upon furnishing payment-receipt information to the Authorised Dealer, **provided that the value of inward remittance for the service does not exceed Ã¢â€šÂ¹10 Lakh** (or foreign currency equivalent). If remittance exceeds Ã¢â€šÂ¹10 Lakh, standard formal EDF filing is required.

### Regulation 4: Manner of Receipt & EDPMS Closure
* **Regulation 4(1):** Receipts and payments must comply with FEMA (Manner of Receipt and Payment) Regulations.
* **Regulation 4(2):** Authorised Dealers may credit or debit an account only after satisfying themselves of transaction bona fides, and must simultaneously update or close the corresponding entry in EDPMS or IDPMS.
* **Ã¢â€šÂ¹10 Lakh Provision under Regulation 4(2) (EDPMS Closure):**
  * For export bills/invoices of value **up to Ã¢â€šÂ¹10 Lakh** (or foreign currency equivalent), the open tracking entry in EDPMS can be closed based directly on a **declaration from the exporter** confirming that payment has been realised (in full or otherwise).
  * Exporters can submit declarations on a **quarterly consolidated basis** for bulk knock-off.
  * AD banks are directed not to levy penal charges for documentation delays on transactions within this Ã¢â€šÂ¹10 Lakh threshold.

---

### Regulation 5: Realisation and Repatriation of Export Proceeds
* **Regulation 5(1)(a) & (b):** The full value of exported goods or services must be realised and repatriated to India within **9 months** from the date of export/shipment (goods) or from the **date of invoice** (services). *(Amended from 15 months by Notification FEMA 23(R)/(1)/2026-RB).*
* **First Proviso to Regulation 5(1) Ã¢â‚¬â€ Indian Rupee (INR) Settlement:** Where exports are invoiced and settled in **Indian Rupees (INR)** (e.g., via Special Rupee Vostro Accounts - SRVA), the permissible realization and repatriation period is extended to **12 months** from the invoice date.
* **AD Bank Powers:** AD Category-I banks possess delegated authority to grant extensions beyond 9 months (or 12 months) upon receiving genuine justification.

---

### Regulation 6: Reduction in Export Realisation (Write-Offs, Fees & Deductions)
* **Regulation 6(1):** Authorised Dealer banks are empowered to approve a reduction in the full export value (short-realization or write-off) where the exporter is unable to realize the full declared value due to genuine commercial reasons (e.g., discount, fee deductions, commercial dispute).
* **Ã¢â€šÂ¹10 Lakh Provision under Regulation 6:**
  * For export bills/invoices where the total value or the shortfall is **up to Ã¢â€šÂ¹10 Lakh** (or foreign currency equivalent), an Authorised Dealer may permit the reduction and regularize/knock off the bill in EDPMS on the basis of a **self-declaration/signed declaration from the exporter** explaining the bona fide commercial reason, without requiring third-party certificates or prior regulatory approval.

---

### Regulation 18: Import Monitoring & IDPMS Thresholds
* **Regulation 18(1)(d):** Governs data entry and tracking for service imports in IDPMS.
* **Ã¢â€šÂ¹10 Lakh Provision under Regulation 18(1)(d):**
  * Authorised Dealer banks are required to enter details of service imports into IDPMS within 5 working days of receipt of documents **ONLY IF**:
    1. The importer is an entity **other than a resident individual**; **AND**
    2. The invoice or contract value **exceeds Ã¢â€šÂ¹10 Lakh** (or foreign currency equivalent).
  * Service imports by resident individuals (regardless of value) and service imports by entities $\le$ Ã¢â€šÂ¹10 Lakh are exempt from mandatory 5-day IDPMS logging.

### 4.0 Evidentiary Classification Taxonomy

To ensure complete legal and audit integrity, every element in this document is categorized under one of four distinct evidentiary tiers:

1. **Tier A Ã¢â‚¬â€ Statutorily Printed Primary Evidence (VERIFIED):**
   Items, text labels, table column headers, checkboxes, declaration text, and footnotes that appear *verbatim* in the official RBI PDF (`NRAR23R16012026_AN.pdf`) or gazetted regulation text.
2. **Tier B Ã¢â‚¬â€ Structural / Form Interpretations (UNRESOLVED BY STATUTE):**
   Classifications regarding whether a field is "mandatory", "conditional", or "optional". The official RBI PDF contains *no asterisks or statutory mandatory/optional tags*. These statuses are logical engineering classifications based on trade type (e.g., Shipping Bill is logically inapplicable to digital services, Internet is checked for digital delivery).
3. **Tier C Ã¢â‚¬â€ Cross-Statutory / External Regulatory Mandates:**
   Requirements originating outside FEMA 23(R)/2026-RB:
   * **Service Accounting Code (SAC):** Printed in Form EDF Section 2B, but defined and mandated under the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 (Section 31 & invoice rules) for zero-rated export classification.
   * **Importer-Exporter Code (IEC):** Administered by DGFT under the Foreign Trade (Development and Regulation) Act and Foreign Trade Policy (FTP). Exempt for certain individual service exporters not claiming FTP benefits.
4. **Tier D Ã¢â‚¬â€ Operational Assumptions & Industry Practice (NON-STATUTORY):**
   Engineering and accounting assumptions necessary for real-world software implementation:
   * **Net Realisable Value & PayPal Fees:** The official PDF prints the label `Net Realisable value`, but the regulation does not define how payment gateway (PayPal/OPGSP) fees are deducted. Treating PayPal merchant discount fees as an allowable variance between gross invoice and Net Realisable Value is an *operational practice* supported by Regulation 6 reduction provisions, not an explicit statutory definition of PayPal.
   * **PayPal's Mode of Realisation:** Classifying PayPal inward remittances under `[ ] Others (advance payment, etc. including transfer/remittance to bank a/c maintained overseas)` is an *operational convention*, as PayPal/PA-CB is not a traditional Letter of Credit (L/C) or Bank Guarantee.
   * **Realisation Clock:** Regulation 5(1)(b) statutorily defines the 9-month realization period "from the date of invoice in the case of services". However, the automated triggering of alerts and grace periods in software is an *application design choice*.
