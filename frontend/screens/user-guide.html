Frequently Asked
What is the EDPMS 21-day rule? expand_more

Under RBI Master Directions, shipping bills must be matched against realization FIRAs within statutory deadlines (ordinarily 9 months for realization, but bank cautionary notifications begin flagging unclosed EDPMS entries). EDFlow reconciles these instantly.

How are bank haircut fees handled? expand_more

FEMA guidelines allow writing off or auto-absorbing intermediary correspondent bank charges up to specified percentage thresholds. EDFlow automates statutory justification without manual bank visits.

Can I invite our Chartered Accountant (CA)? expand_more

Yes. CA collaborator seats are unlimited. They receive read-only review rights to sign off on 15CA/CB documentation directly inside the EDF Pack dossier module.
